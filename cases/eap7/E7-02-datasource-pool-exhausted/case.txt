## Topology
Single EAP 7.4 instance, standalone-full.xml, one Oracle XA datasource used by an EJB
layer. Roughly 400 concurrent users at peak.

## Configuration
The datasources subsystem block is in configs/subsystem-datasources.snippet.xml.
max-pool-size is 20; blocking-timeout-wait-millis is 30000.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Start the application under normal daytime load.
2. Within 20-40 minutes of peak traffic, requests begin to queue.
3. Every request that touches the database then fails.

## Expected behavior
Connections are returned to the pool and requests complete.

## Actual behavior
The connection pool is exhausted and never recovers without a restart. Thread dumps show
worker threads parked in the pool's blocking wait.

## Error messages
javax.resource.ResourceException: IJ000453: Unable to get managed connection for java:/OracleDS
IJ000655: No managed connections available within configured blocking timeout (30000 [ms])

## Logs / stack traces
"default task-118" #118 waiting on condition
   at jdk.internal.misc.Unsafe.park(java.base@11.0.22/Native Method)
   at org.jboss.jca.core.connectionmanager.pool.mcp.SemaphoreConcurrentLinkedDequeManagedConnectionPool.getConnection(...)

## Frequency
under load only, every business day

## What the customer has already tried
Raised max-pool-size from 20 to 50; the exhaustion took longer to appear but still happened.
No leak detection (cached-connection-manager debug) has been enabled yet.
