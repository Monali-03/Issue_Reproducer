## Topology
Single EAP 7.4 instance terminating TLS directly on the https listener. A third-party
partner calls the SOAP endpoint over HTTPS. No load balancer in front.

## Configuration
Elytron key-store / key-manager / server-ssl-context, with the https-listener bound to it.
See configs/subsystem-elytron-ssl.snippet.xml. The keystore is JKS, RSA 2048, SHA256.
Nothing was modified on the EAP side; the partner upgraded their client.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Partner client opens a TLS connection to https://eap-host:8443/ws/partner.
2. The handshake is attempted.
3. The connection is closed before any HTTP request is sent.

## Expected behavior
The handshake completes and the SOAP request is processed.

## Actual behavior
Every connection from that partner fails at the handshake. Other clients still work.
