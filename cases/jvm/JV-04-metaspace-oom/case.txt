## Topology
A rules engine that compiles and loads generated classes at runtime. Each rule reload
creates a new classloader. Reloads happen roughly every 10 minutes.

## Configuration
JVM flags are in configs/jvm-args.txt. Metaspace is capped at 256m; heap is 1g.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Start the service.
2. Trigger a rule reload every 10 minutes.
3. Watch Metaspace committed size with jcmd VM.metaspace.

## Expected behavior
Metaspace stays flat: the classloader from the previous generation is collected and its
class metadata released.

## Actual behavior
Metaspace grows with every reload and is never released. After about 90 reloads the JVM
throws OutOfMemoryError: Metaspace. Heap itself stays healthy, so heap-based monitoring
never alerts.

## Error messages
java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Metaspace

## Logs / stack traces
[gc,metaspace] GC(451) Metaspace: 261054K(262144K)->261054K(262144K)

## Frequency
always, after roughly 90 reloads (about 15 hours)

## What the customer has already tried
Raised -XX:MaxMetaspaceSize from 128m to 256m; it doubled the time to failure. They
suspect the generated classes are being retained but have not confirmed by what.
